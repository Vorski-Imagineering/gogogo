We use the gogogo dev process here. The spec I am building says in its Design, in these words: "With those gone `tests/test_journey_background.py` holds no tests, so the module goes too." My change takes that module out, and the check that compares the tests my change touched reports it as NOT LICENSED. What does the process say I do now? Answer in two or three sentences, in the process's own terms, change nothing, and end with exactly one of these lines: `Next: stop for a person`, `Next: restore the module`, `Next: carry on and merge`.
