We use the gogogo human-help process here. A card in the Human!Help! column stopped with reason=hard-stop: the change needs the owner's approval under one of the repo's ask-before-changing rules, and no comment answers it. Besides naming the approval and recommending an answer, what does the process say the question to the owner contains? Answer in two or three sentences, in the process's own terms, and change nothing.
