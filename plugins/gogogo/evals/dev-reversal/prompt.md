We use the gogogo dev process here. In round 3 of a code review, a finding asks to undo a correction that round 1 made. It shows no failure of the spec, nothing that broke, no bug and no risk. What does the process do with that finding, and what word is it recorded under? Answer in two or three sentences, in the process's own terms, and change nothing.
