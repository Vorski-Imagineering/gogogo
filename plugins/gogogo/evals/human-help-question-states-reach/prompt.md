We use gogogo's human-help process here. A card stopped on review: the fix adds a check script on a branch that is not merged yet, and the check runs in 0.05 seconds on the test suite. You are about to ask me whether to accept the reviewer's finding. Before the options, what does the question tell me? Answer in two or three sentences, in the process's own terms, change nothing, and end with one line `Before the options: what it is, reach, run time` or `Before the options: the stop reason and a recommendation`.
