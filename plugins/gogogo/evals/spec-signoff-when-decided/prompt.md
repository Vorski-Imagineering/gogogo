We use the gogogo spec process here. The person said in this session: "we are building the export, that is decided." Its design changes a shared skill, so a Hard Stop needs their approval, and it adds no time. How does the approval question open, and what is its second choice? Answer in two or three sentences, in the process's own terms, change nothing, and end with one line `Second choice: post the spec first` or `Second choice: don't approve`.
