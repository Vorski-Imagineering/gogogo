We use the gogogo dev process here. Round 1 of a code review found that the Stop button is hidden on a short phone, and the correction measured the header's height. Round 2 confirms Stop now stays in view on that phone, and finds a different case in the correction's new lines: a two-line question makes the header taller than the measurement. A real run's record shows a two-line question on that page. Is this another attempt at the round-1 finding, and how does the report's Findings table mark it? Answer in two or three sentences, in the process's own terms, change nothing, and end with one line `Next: a new finding` or `Next: attempt 2`.
